## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description
Expected Solution: Create a digital marketplace that:

• Connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
• Provides logistics support.
• Uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization.

Benefits:

• Better prices for farmers.
• Lower prices for consumers.
• Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.

## Proposed Solution
FarmConnect is an AI-powered digital marketplace that directly connects farmers, FPOs, consumers, and bulk buyers. It eliminates unnecessary middlemen, helping farmers earn better profits while consumers get fresh agricultural products at lower prices.

Key Features:

Direct farmer-to-consumer selling.

AI-based demand and price prediction.

Smart logistics and route optimization.

Secure online payments.

Real-time product availability and price updates.

Bulk purchasing for retailers and businesses.

## Technical Approach
Frontend: React.js / Flutter

Backend: Python / Django

Database: MySQL / PostgreSQL

AI/ML: Python, TensorFlow

Maps: Google Maps API

Payments: UPI / Razorpay

Cloud: AWS

Workflow: Farmer Registration → Product Listing → AI Analysis → Buyer Matching → Order Placement → Payment → Delivery.



## Feasibility and Viability

The platform can be developed using existing technologies and digital payment systems.

Challenges:

Limited digital literacy.

Internet connectivity issues.

Transportation and storage problems.

Solutions:

Regional language support.

Simple mobile application.

Partnerships with logistics providers.

FPO-assisted registration.
## Impact and Benefits
Farmers: Higher income and direct market access.

Consumers: Lower prices and fresh products.

Environment: Reduced food wastage and transportation inefficiencies.

Economy: Improved rural development and agricultural growth.
